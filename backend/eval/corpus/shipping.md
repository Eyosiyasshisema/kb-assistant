# Shipping and Delivery

Abyssinia Roast Co. delivers across Ethiopia. We do not ship internationally.

## Delivery times
- Addis Ababa: 1 to 2 business days after dispatch.
- Other Ethiopian cities (for example Hawassa, Bahir Dar, Mekelle): 3 to 5 business days after dispatch.
- Business days are Monday to Saturday. There are no deliveries on Sundays or public holidays.
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM Addis Ababa time on a business day are dispatched the same day.

## Shipping fees
- Orders of 2,500 ETB or more ship free anywhere in Ethiopia.
- Orders under 2,500 ETB pay 120 ETB shipping within Addis Ababa and 250 ETB to other cities.

## Tracking and failed deliveries
- You receive a tracking link by SMS and email when your order ships.
- Our courier makes 2 delivery attempts. After the second failed attempt the parcel returns to our warehouse, and re-delivery costs 80 ETB.
