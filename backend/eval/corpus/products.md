# Our Coffees

We sell four single-origin whole-bean coffees from smallholder cooperatives in Ethiopia.

- Yirgacheffe Washed: light roast with floral and citrus notes. 250 g bag: 520 ETB.
- Sidamo Natural: medium roast with berry and chocolate notes. 250 g bag: 500 ETB.
- Guji Natural: light-medium roast with peach and honey notes. 250 g bag: 560 ETB.
- Harrar Longberry: dark roast with wine-like, spicy notes. 250 g bag: 450 ETB.

## Freshness
Every order is roasted fresh to order, within 48 hours before it ships. The roast date is printed on every bag. For best flavour, drink the coffee within 6 weeks of the roast date.

## Grinding
Coffee is sold as whole beans. We can grind it for you at no extra cost: choose filter, espresso or moka pot at checkout.
