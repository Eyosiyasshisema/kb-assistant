# Returns and Refunds

## Unopened coffee
You can return an unopened bag within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened bags cannot be returned, unless the coffee is damaged or we sent the wrong item.

## Damaged or wrong items
If your order arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery and include a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer.

## How refunds work
- Refunds go back to the original payment method.
- We process refunds within 5 business days after we receive the returned item.
- Return shipping is free for damaged or wrong items. For any other return, the customer pays return shipping.

## Starting a return
Email support@abyssiniaroast.example with your order number.
