# Coffee Subscriptions

## Plans
- Starter: 1 bag (250 g) per month, 480 ETB per month.
- Enthusiast: 2 bags (250 g each) per month, 900 ETB per month.
- Family: 4 bags (250 g each) per month, 1,700 ETB per month.
- Shipping is free on all subscription plans.

## First-order discount
New subscribers get 10% off their first month.

## Changes, skipping and pausing
- You can skip a delivery, change your blend, or change your plan up to 3 days before your next shipment date. Changes made later apply to the following month.
- You can pause your subscription for up to 2 months at a time.
- You can cancel at any time. There is no cancellation fee.

## Billing
You are billed on the same day each month that you first subscribed. If a payment fails we retry up to 3 times over 5 days, then pause the subscription.
