# Brewing Guide

## Pour-over (V60)
Use 15 g of medium-fine coffee for 240 ml of water. Water temperature: 92 to 96 °C. Total brew time: 2:30 to 3:00 minutes.

## Espresso
Use 18 g of ground coffee and aim for 36 g of espresso in 25 to 30 seconds.

## Jebena (traditional Ethiopian method)
Use finely ground coffee, about 1 tablespoon per small cup. Bring the water to a boil, add the coffee, and let it rise and settle three times. Let it rest for 1 minute before pouring.

## Storage
Keep coffee in an airtight container in a cool, dark place. Do not refrigerate or freeze it.
