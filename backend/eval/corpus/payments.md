# Payments and Pricing

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Telebirr, CBE Birr and bank transfer. Cash on delivery is available in Addis Ababa only, for orders up to 3,000 ETB.

## Prices and invoices
All prices are in Ethiopian birr (ETB) and include 15% VAT. A VAT invoice is available on request within 7 days of your order.

## Promo codes
One promo code can be used per order. Promo codes cannot be combined with the 10% first-month subscription discount.
